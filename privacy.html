Privacy Policy

算咯 · 隐私政策
Effective Date: September 15, 2026

1. Overview

StatLife ("the App") is designed to perform statistical analysis using data provided by the user. We value your privacy and aim to keep the App's data processing simple and transparent.

「算咯」（以下简称“本 App”）是一款使用用户提供的数据进行 统计分析的工具。我们重视用户隐私，并致力于让 App 中的数据处理 保持简单、透明。

2. Data You Enter

Users may manually enter numerical data, labels, variable names, group names, or other information required to perform an analysis.

StatLife does not require you to create an account. Data entered for statistical analysis is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to a StatLife server.

用户可以手动输入分析所需的数值、标签、变量名称、分组名称及其他信息。 「算咯」不要求用户创建账户。用于统计分析的数据在设备本地处理， 不会上传至「算咯」的服务器。

3. CSV Files

StatLife may allow you to select and import CSV files from locations available through Apple's file selection interface.

CSV data selected for analysis is processed locally on your device. StatLife does not upload the contents of your CSV files to a StatLife server.

「算咯」支持通过 Apple 提供的文件选择界面选择并导入 CSV 文件。 被选择用于分析的 CSV 数据在设备本地处理， 「算咯」不会将 CSV 文件内容上传至自己的服务器。

4. Purchases and Subscriptions

StatLife may offer optional Pro features through in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Purchases, subscription billing, and related transaction processing are handled by Apple through the App Store. StatLife does not directly collect or process your payment card information.

「算咯」可能通过 App 内购买或订阅提供可选的 Pro 功能。 购买、订阅计费及相关交易处理由 Apple 通过 App Store 完成。 「算咯」不会直接收集或处理你的银行卡或支付卡信息。

5. Accounts and StatLife Servers

StatLife currently does not provide a user account system and does not operate a server for uploading or storing the statistical data you enter into the App.

「算咯」目前不提供用户账户系统，也不运营用于上传或存储 用户在 App 中输入的统计数据的服务器。

6. Third-Party Services

Certain App functionality, including purchases and subscriptions, may rely on services provided by Apple. Your use of those services may be subject to Apple's applicable privacy policies and terms.

App 的部分功能（包括购买和订阅）可能使用 Apple 提供的服务。 用户使用这些服务时，可能同时受到 Apple 相应隐私政策及条款的约束。

7. Children's Privacy

StatLife is a general-purpose statistical analysis tool and is not specifically directed to children. The App does not knowingly operate a system for collecting personal information from children.

「算咯」是一款通用统计分析工具，并非专门面向儿童设计。 本 App 不运营以收集儿童个人信息为目的的系统。

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when StatLife's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

如果「算咯」的功能或数据处理方式发生变化，我们可能更新本隐私政策。 更新后的版本将在本页面公布，并标明新的生效日期。
